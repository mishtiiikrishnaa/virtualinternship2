# assumptions and decisions log (week 1)

- D1. teaching language: python. decided because python has readable syntax, runs in the browser with no installation, and suits adult beginners. alternatives (scratch, javascript) rejected for reasons given in section 1 of the report.
- D2. delivery mode: virtual, browser tools only (google colab / browser editor). decided so learners need no installs and so the plan fits a remote internship in ahmedabad.
- D3. module length: 10 modules, 30 teaching hours. decided to keep scope inside a single focused cohort and leave the capstone time for building, not just listening.
- A1. cohort size: 20 learners per cohort. assumption — keeps pair programming and tutor feedback feasible.
- A2. staffing: 1 lead instructor + 2 tutors for 5 weeks. assumption — matches the 120 hour effort total in section 8.
- A3. baseline date: 5 november 2026, first working day on or after the report date (4 november 2026). all section 9 dates are planned and ascend from this baseline, skipping weekends and govind jayanti (25 november 2026, hindi month holiday — checked as a probable west india public holiday; if it falls differently for the venue, shift the launch milestone by one working day).
- A4. budget: uses free tiers where possible, so costs are mostly people time. amounts are indicative planning figures, not quotes.
- A5. accessibility baseline: captions on all videos, colour-blind-safe palette in figures, keyboard-navigable colab. additional needs would add cost.
