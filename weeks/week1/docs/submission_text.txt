I planned a 30-hour programming fundamentals module for adults who have never written code, taught in Python through the browser so nothing needs installing. The plan covers who the learners are, what research says about common beginner challenges and what helps, seven measurable learning outcomes mapped to Bloom levels and to modules and assessments, ten modules from thinking in steps through variables, choices, loops, functions, lists, files, and debugging, ending in a personal mini-project, plus teaching methods, assessment, resources, timeline, risks, and success measures. It includes six diagrams: a curriculum flowchart, a learner journey, a Gantt timeline, a session shape, an assessment map, and a resource chart. Dates in the timeline are planned from a 5 November 2026 baseline, skipping weekends and a public holiday, with 120 planned hours matching the resource table. Key decisions were Python over Scratch and JavaScript for readability and browser access, pairs and Parsons problems to lower cognitive load, frequent short feedback, and a rubric that rewards explanation as well as a working program.
